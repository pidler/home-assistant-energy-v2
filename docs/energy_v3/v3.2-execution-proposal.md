# ENERGY V3.2 execution proposal

`execution_proposal.py` is a pure shadow translation layer. It consumes an existing
V3 controller decision and describes the protocol setpoint and counterpart interlock
that physical execution would require. It has no Home Assistant or AppDaemon API,
does not call a service, and cannot grant execution authority.

## Existing experimental evidence

The following supervised production experiments are accepted as physical evidence:

- SolaX VPP `-1200 W` discharged at approximately 1050-1080 W, `+1200 W`
  charged at approximately 1265 W, and a zero request was exercised.
- SolaX returned to Self Use after an approximately 30-second timeout; the
  90-second autorepeat path was also physically exercised.
- DEYE register 1109 raw `+100` discharged at approximately 1198 W and raw
  `-100` charged at approximately 1195 W.
- DEYE uses a 12 kW rated-power basis and signed 0.1-percent register steps.
- A 30-second DEYE watchdog expiry disabled Remote Mode.

These observations verify direction, scaling and timeout behavior for the tested
conditions. They do not verify a safe zero-power hold for the other inverter, an
exclusive-writer handoff, all failure modes, or complete closed-loop control.

## Translation

HAEO is positive discharge and negative charge. SolaX VPP uses the opposite sign
in watts and has a 100 W entity step. DEYE register 1109 keeps the HAEO direction
and uses raw signed 0.1-percent units; at 12 kW, raw `100` represents 1200 W.

The first active version remains single-owner. Two simultaneous nonzero targets
are retained in diagnostics but are not translated to commands. Opposite directions
remain a cross-transfer rejection.

## Fail-closed readiness

Every active proposal requires exclusive ownership and a separately verified
zero-power interlock for the other inverter. Charging remains blocked until the
15 kW PCC import guard exists in the controller/runtime contract. Commanded SolaX
discharge remains blocked until the controller implements the 30% trading reserve.
That reserve does not apply to native Self Use, so normal household supply below
30% is unaffected.

The existing controller continues to own target validity, physical SOC protection,
the 9.8 kW operational export ceiling, export authorization, telemetry and fault
evidence. `ExecutionProposal` reports controller rejection instead of repeating or
overriding those decisions.
